About

With over 16 years of experience in the software industry, Data Rellie provides innovative software, data and AI solutions. We are customer focused, data-driven and we track and assess success based mostly on quantitative metrics but also on qualitative information.

We work thoughout all the life cycle of a solution. From analyzing the business case with data, to running and monitoring software in production, including all the software development life cycle. We specialize in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science and data engineering solutions and operations, relying heavily in cloud computing and CI/CD pipelines.

We work with the private and the public sector, on multiple industries and also on research-oriented projects covering from the design and implementation of technological solutions to the submission of research papers to journals and conferences.

Services We Offer

🧠 AI & Machine Learning

We can support you throughout the integration of solutions with IA engines and LLMs and through the full life cycle of machine learning systems: From data preparation, model training and evaluation, to the deployment and monitoring of the system once in production.

🔬 Data Science & Analytics

Our data science services help you extract valuable insights from your data. Either to answer specific questions or to report automatically on your KPIs or particular aspects of a solution. It is important to back ideas and business cases with data before investing significant resources in them. Data-driven decisions result in less risks and less ambiguity.

👨🏻‍💻 Software Development

We offer software engineering services like custom development or advice on software engineering best practices, always with a focus on easily maintainable and robust code which implies automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, load testing and performance optimization.

We build robust and scalable data pipelines to collect, process, and store your data for analytics and training purposes. We can also advise on how to define your data strategy so that it is not only a source of value for your company but it also it maintainable and expandable.

We are cloud native and we can help you build cost-effective and scalable system architectures in the cloud. We hold AWS Solutions Architecture and AWS Machine Learning certifications.
